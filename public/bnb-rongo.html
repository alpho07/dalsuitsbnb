Why stay with us in Rongo

Rongo has plenty of passing traffic: it sits on the Kisii–Migori highway, the main road linking Kisii, Migori and the Tanzania border at Isebania. DAL Suits is set back at Nyarach Kobura, so you get the convenience of town without the noise of the highway.

We are a family-run business, not an agency. The host answers the phone, hands over the keys and stays reachable for your whole stay.

Who stays with us

  • Couples looking for a quiet weekend away
  • Families visiting relatives in Rongo and Migori County
  • Parents and guests attending events at Rongo University
  • Business travellers, NGO and county staff on assignment
  • Anyone who simply needs a secluded place to rest

Getting to DAL Suits

From Kisii, follow the Kisii–Migori highway south to Rongo. From Migori, head north on the same highway; Rongo is about 30 km from Migori town. Our pin in Google Maps is labelled “DAL Suits BnB” at Nyarach Kobura, and we can guide you to the gate by phone or WhatsApp.
