BnB near Homa Bay: stay in Rongo

DAL Suits BnB is in Rongo Town, Migori County, not in Homa Bay itself. For trips that cover Homa Bay County as well as Migori, Kisii or Rongo University, Rongo makes a convenient and quiet place to sleep.

Why stay in Rongo for a Homa Bay trip

Rongo is connected to Homa Bay by road and sits on the Kisii–Migori highway, so you can split your days between Homa Bay County and Migori County without changing accommodation. Evenings are quiet at Nyarach Kobura, with fast Wi-Fi and Netflix in every suite.

  • The Lake Victoria waterfront and Homa Bay town
  • Simbi Nyaima crater lake near Kendu Bay
  • Business in Ndhiwa, Rangwe and Oyugis

Questions

Is DAL Suits BnB in Homa Bay?

No. We're in Rongo Town, Migori County. Many guests visiting Homa Bay County stay with us because Rongo also connects easily to Migori, Kisii and Rongo University.

How long is the drive from Rongo to Homa Bay?

It depends on traffic and road works; tell us your plans on WhatsApp and we'll advise on the best route and timing.

Can I book for a group travelling together?

Yes. With four suites across two blocks we can host small groups; tell us how many guests.
