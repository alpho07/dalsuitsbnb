Where the university is

Rongo University's main campus is on Kitere Hill, outside Rongo Town and just off the Kisii–Migori highway. DAL Suits is at Nyarach Kobura in Rongo Town, so you can stay in town, close to shops, transport and food, and reach campus by car, taxi or boda boda.

Book early for graduation week

Accommodation around Rongo fills up quickly around graduation ceremonies and reporting days. If you know your dates, send them to us on WhatsApp as soon as you can and we'll hold a suite while we confirm.

Questions

How far is DAL Suits from Rongo University?

We're in Rongo Town, and the university is on Kitere Hill, a short drive away. Call or WhatsApp us and we'll suggest the best way to get there from the suite.

Can families of graduates stay together?

Yes. Each suite has two bedrooms and sleeps up to 4 when you book the whole suite; bigger families can take two suites.

Do you offer longer stays for university staff?

Yes. Ask us for a weekly or monthly rate.
