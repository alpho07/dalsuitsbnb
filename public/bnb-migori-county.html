BnB in Migori County: a quiet base in Rongo

Rongo is Migori County's northern gateway. DAL Suits BnB gives visitors to the county a calm, comfortable base, with easy road access south to Awendo, Migori town and the Tanzania border.

On the main road through the county

The Kisii–Migori highway runs through Rongo, Ranen, Awendo, Migori, Isebania and Sirare. Staying in Rongo puts you at the northern end of that corridor, about 30 km from Migori town, with Kisii to the north.

Things to see in Migori County

  • Thimlich Ohinga, the UNESCO World Heritage stone enclosures near Macalder
  • Gogo Falls on the Kuja River
  • The Lake Victoria shoreline at Muhuru Bay
  • Market days and the gold-mining heritage around Rongo and Macalder

Who stays with us

Visitors to family in Rongo, Awendo and Uriri; county, NGO and business travellers working across Migori; and couples and families who want somewhere quieter than a town-centre hotel.
